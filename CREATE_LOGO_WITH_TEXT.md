## Design Guidelines

### Text Specifications
- **Font**: Sans-serif (modern, clean)
- **Weight**: Bold or Semi-bold
- **Color**: 
  - White (if dark background)
  - Dark gray/black (if light background)
  - Or match your brand colors

### Layout Tips
1. **Keep it simple**: Icon + text, nothing more
2. **Readable**: Text should be clear even at small sizes
3. **Balanced**: Icon and text should be proportional
4. **Padding**: Leave some space around edges
5. **Contrast**: Ensure good contrast between elements

### Color Schemes (Based on Your Flame Icon)
- **Option 1**: Green flame + white text on dark background
- **Option 2**: Green flame + dark text on white background
- **Option 3**: Colorful flame + gradient text

## Quick Canva Tutorial

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Open Canva** → https://www.canva.com/
2. **Click "Create a design"**
3. **Select "Custom size"** → Enter 512 x 512 pixels
4. **Click "Create new design"**

5. **Add Background** (optional):
   - Click "Elements" → Search "background"
   - Or use solid color
   - Or keep transparent

6. **Upload Your Icon**:
   - Click "Uploads" → "Upload files"
   - Select `assets/images/logo-new.png`
   - Drag it onto canvas
   - Resize to fit nicely (leave room for text)

7. **Add Text**:
   - Click "Text" → "Add a heading"
   - Type "ECO SUDAR" or "Eco Sudar"
   - Choose font (try: Montserrat, Poppins, Raleway)
   - Make it bold
   - Adjust size
   - Position below or beside icon

8. **Adjust Colors**:
   - Select text → Change color to match your brand
   - Ensure good contrast

9. **Download**:
   - Click "Share" → "Download"
   - Format: PNG
   - Check "Transparent background" (if needed)
   - Click "Download"

10. **Save**:
    - Save downloaded file as `icon-512x512.png`
    - Move to `play-store-assets/` folder
    - Replace existing file
